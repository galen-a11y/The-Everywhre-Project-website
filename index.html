Who we are

We don't wait for people to come to us. We go to them.

The Everywhere Project brings outreach, hot meals and health support to neighbors in Philadelphia experiencing homelessness, food insecurity and substance use. We meet people where they are, treat everyone with dignity and help connect them to care.

That looks like a hot meal at LOVE Park on a Wednesday night, naloxone and wound care in Kensington on a Saturday, and a warm coat when the weather turns.

Overdoses are preventable. Harm reduction is love.

Our story

Born from necessity. Built on showing up.

What started as a few people with food and naloxone has grown into one of Philadelphia's most active harm reduction organizations.

Why we started

In the winter of 2020, months into the pandemic, overdoses in Philadelphia were higher than they'd been in years and most outreach had stopped going out. Our neighbors still needed a meal, naloxone and someone to check on them.

So we showed up. A few people with a car full of supplies became a weekly table, and then two. By May 2021 we were a 501(c)(3), and our first week at LOVE Park we served 30 neighbors.

We believe that people need people, and none of us can get through life alone.

Today you'll find us at LOVE Park every Wednesday and in Kensington every Saturday, with hot meals, naloxone, wound care and warm clothes. In 2025 we rescued 178,500 pounds of food that would otherwise have been thrown away, and about 1,000 volunteers showed up to help. We're now the #2 safer-use supplier in Philadelphia, and we've never missed an outreach night.

Mission

We show up for neighbors in Philadelphia experiencing homelessness, food insecurity and substance use, with outreach, hot meals and health support, meeting people where they are with dignity and helping connect them to care.

Vision

A world without overdoses, where substance use is no longer stigmatized and every person receives the support and dignity they deserve.
